# RecordForge Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Notice:** this is a template pending legal review. It follows the
> well-known Apache-style CLA structure; have a lawyer look at it before
> enforcing it on contributors.

Thank you for contributing to RecordForge. This agreement clarifies the
intellectual-property terms for Contributions to the project, which operates
an open-core model: the repository is GPL-3.0-or-later, and the maintainers
also ship commercial "official" builds that include the same code. A CLA is
required so both the GPL distribution *and* the commercial distribution can
lawfully include your work.

By submitting a Contribution (code, documentation, tests, artwork, or other
material) to this repository — via pull request, issue, email, or otherwise —
you agree to the following:

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"**: the individual or legal entity making the Contribution.
- **"Contribution"**: any work of authorship you intentionally submit to the
  project.
- **"Maintainers"**: Prestige Tech and the RecordForge project maintainers.

## 2. Copyright license

You grant the Maintainers a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge,
royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to:

- reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly
  perform, sublicense, and distribute your Contribution; and
- do so under **any license terms**, including GPL-3.0-or-later for the open
  repository *and* proprietary/commercial terms for official builds and
  related services.

Your Contribution remains yours; you retain full copyright ownership and may
use it anywhere else for any purpose.

## 3. Patent license

You grant the Maintainers and recipients of the software a perpetual,
worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as
stated here) patent license to make, use, sell, and distribute your
Contribution, where such license applies only to patent claims you own that
are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or combined with the
project. This license terminates for any entity that files a patent claim
alleging the Contribution or the project infringes a patent.

## 4. Representations

You represent that:

- you are legally entitled to grant these licenses (if your employer has
  rights to your work, you have permission or a signed corporate CLA);
- each Contribution is your original work, or you have disclosed all
  third-party licenses and attributions it contains;
- you will not knowingly submit code under terms incompatible with the GPL.

## 5. No obligation

You are not expected to provide support for your Contribution, except as you
voluntarily offer. The Maintainers may decline or remove any Contribution.

## 6. How it is signed

The CLA Assistant GitHub app comments on your first pull request; posting the
requested confirmation (e.g. "I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign
the CLA") records your acceptance in `.clabot` / the assistant's store.
