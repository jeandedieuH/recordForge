# RecordForge 2.1.5

## Highlights

- **Keystroke badges, upgraded** — shortcut presses captured during recording
  now render as styled keycap badges in the editor preview and in exports,
  with platform-correct modifier glyphs (⌘ on macOS, Win/Super elsewhere).
  A new **Keys** panel controls position, theme, size, stack depth, and how
  long badges stay on screen, with a live sample of exactly what ships.
  Recordings made before this release still display their shortcuts.
  Keystroke badges are a Pro feature.
- **Smarter Smart Cut** — silence removal now analyzes the audio against the
  recording's own noise floor, shows detected silences on a waveform overview
  and as a list you can review and toggle one by one, and previews the cut
  ranges right on the timeline before you commit. Applying is a single
  undoable step, and Smart Cut warns you if a locked track would desync the
  timeline or if a cut would remove nearly all audible audio. Smart Cut is a
  Pro feature.

## Fixes

- Fixed waveforms and video thumbnails in the editor sometimes getting stuck
  on "loading" forever.
