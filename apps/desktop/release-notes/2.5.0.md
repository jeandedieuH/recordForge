# RecordForge 2.5.0

## Highlights

- **Dynamic camera layouts.** A new **Camera layouts** lane under the camera
  track switches the camera between picture-in-picture, fullscreen,
  side-by-side and hidden at any point in the timeline. Drag a segment to move
  it, trim its edges, double-click a segment to cycle its layout, and shape it
  in the inspector (corner, size, shape, custom position, easing, transition
  lengths). Outside a segment the camera keeps the layout you set on the clip,
  so the lane only takes over where you want it to.
- **Video b-roll.** Import a video and drop it onto the timeline (or use **Add
  at playhead** in the Assets panel) to composite it over your screen
  recording. B-roll gets position, size, fit, corner radius, border, shadow,
  fade/scale/slide in and out, and fullscreen mode, plus its own embedded
  audio with volume, mute and fades. Clips show a filmstrip from the imported
  file.
- **Custom media tracks.** **Track → Media track** in the timeline toolbar adds
  a lane that holds b-roll video, images and extra audio. Tracks are layers:
  order decides what sits on top, mute hides a track's visuals and silences its
  audio, and lock, rename and drag-reorder work as they do everywhere else.

## Improvements

- **Gaps render on the GPU.** Timelines with a hole between clips (easy to
  create once b-roll and custom tracks exist) now render on the fast
  compositor, painting the canvas background for the uncovered spans, instead
  of falling back to the compatibility renderer.
- **Layout panel.** The Camera layouts card in the Layout panel lists every
  segment with its layout picker, works at the playhead for adding new ones,
  and can clear the whole lane. Selecting a segment in the timeline opens a
  dedicated inspector for its settings.

## Known issues

- The compatibility export renderer draws b-roll at a fixed rect and camera
  layouts at their static position: animated layers and layout transitions are
  reported as render notes ("Rendered without …") rather than silently
  differing from the preview.
- B-roll clips have no waveform for their embedded audio, and audio beds on
  custom media tracks show a waveform only once the asset's derivative job has
  finished.
- **Minimum version:** projects that use custom media tracks or video b-roll
  clips can only be opened by 2.5.0 or newer. Older builds reject those clip
  and track kinds instead of dropping them, so keep a copy before opening a
  project elsewhere. The camera layout lane itself is never written to the
  project file, so it disappears harmlessly in older versions and returns when
  the project is opened in 2.5.0 again.

## Internal

- ADR 022 unifies the visual layer stack: camera bubbles, b-roll and
  media-track video cross the render-plan boundary as one
  `RenderPlanOverlay` record (with `role`, `fit`, `radius` and an optional
  keyframe track) and draw in `layerOrder`, the middle band in timeline track
  order.
- The camera layout lane (`TimelineState.cameraLayouts`) resolves through
  `resolveCameraLayoutAt` for the preview and samples into
  `RenderPlanCameraLayout` keyframes for export, so preview and file agree.
  Its anchor track (`kind: "camera-layouts"`) is created on load, stripped on
  save and cannot be deleted.
- Declared mid-timeline gaps are spliced as black decode sources with a
  `has_screen` flag, so the composite pass paints background-only frames; a
  trailing gap stays a freeze-frame tail.
- Media-track and b-roll exports mix embedded audio through the same audio
  track graph as recording streams, and mute/solo metadata follows the render
  plan.
