# RecordForge 2.5.7

## Improvements

- **Frame-rate-consistent cinema motion blur & wider shutter sampling.**
  - Motion blur exposure is now calibrated against a 60 ms reference shutter
    window (`max(1000 / fps, 60)` ms) across 24–120 fps so presets produce the
    same visual trail length at 30 fps and 60 fps instead of halving exposure at
    higher frame rates (**Subtle 15 ms**, **Natural 30 ms**, **Cinematic
    45 ms**, **Dynamic 60 ms**), with an effective-exposure `ms` badge in the
    **Motion Blur** header.
  - Tap budgets scale up for wider 60 ms sweeps: the GPU compositor and live
    WebGL preview shader now integrate up to 32 tapered bilinear taps (up from
    24), the compatibility (FFmpeg) renderer blends up to 9 tapered `zoompan` +
    `mix` branches (up from 7), and cursor motion blur accumulates up to 32
    sub-frame poses along the smoothed cursor spline.
  - Clicking **Preview** inside **Motion Blur** (now available in both the
    **Layout** panel and the compact **Focus & Zoom** inspector) only jumps to
    motion sources whose blur channels are enabled, and automatically enables
    live zoom preview when a selected zoom block is in unzoomed framing mode.
- **Refined cursor outlines & dedicated stroke opacity control.**
  - Default and recommended cursor stroke width is now a crisper `1.0px` (down
    from `2.0px`), and every built-in cursor theme and Look preset caps cursor
    outlines at `1.5px`.
  - **Cursor → Advanced** now includes an independent **Stroke opacity** slider
    alongside **Stroke Width** and **Cursor opacity**, and inner badge/detail
    paths on composite cursors (`copy`, `context-menu`, `alias`, `pen`, `pin`,
    and others) now honor `strokeOpacity`.

## Fixes

- **Smooth cursor blur during zoom transitions & instant first-move trails.**
  The cursor motion-blur raster cache now keys off a supersampled,
  zoom-independent reference scale (`>= 2×`), pre-warms while the cursor is
  stationary, and deduplicates in-flight SVG decodes while keeping the previous
  bitmap visible during appearance changes — eliminating cache churn during zoom
  ramps and the single-frame flicker on the first fast cursor flick.
- **Resilient live screen motion-blur preview.** The WebGL shutter overlay now
  retains its drawing buffer across paused, scrubbed, and slider-tuned repaints,
  redraws when the underlying video finishes seeking or decoding, and falls back
  to a fresh Canvas 2D element with exact cumulative `source-over` weighted
  blending if WebGL loses context or fails video texture upload.
- **Zoom segment boundary motion blur in export.** `window_needs_zoom` now checks
  the motion-blur shutter endpoints (`t ± shutter/2`) so boundary frames whose
  center timestamp is still at `1×` are routed through the zoom blur pipeline
  when their exposure window overlaps an adjacent zoom ramp.

## Internal

- `packages/editor-core/src/motion-blur.ts` adds `MOTION_BLUR_REFERENCE_EXPOSURE_MS`
  (`60`), `motionBlurShutterBaseMs`, `motionBlurShutterMs`, and
  `motionBlurEffectiveShutterMs`, mirrored in Rust by
  `camera::motion_blur_shutter_base_ms` across `exports/camera.rs` and
  `exports/cursor.rs`.
- `useTimelineStore` hoists `zoomPreviewActive` / `setZoomPreviewActive` into the
  timeline store (reconciled across selection changes and undo/redo) so
  `MotionBlurControls` and `TimelineView` coordinate zoom-framing preview state.
- `packages/cursor-core/src/assets.ts` and `packages/cursor-engine/assets.json`
  propagate `{strokeOpacity}` across all secondary badge paths; rebuilt
  `packages/cursor-core/wasm/cursor_engine_bg.wasm`.
- Refreshed `golden/effects` and `golden/effects-gpu` benchmark fixtures and
  added unit coverage in `screen-motion-blur-overlay.test.ts`,
  `cursor-motion-blur-canvas.test.ts`, `motion-blur-controls.test.ts`,
  `timeline-store.load.test.ts`, `cursor-inspector.test.ts`, and
  `packages/editor-core/src/looks.test.ts`.
- Workspace version bump to 2.5.7 across all packages; versions earlier than
  2.5.7 marked unsupported in `SECURITY.md`.
