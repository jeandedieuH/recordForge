# RecordForge 2.5.6

## Highlights

- **Live motion blur in the editor & cinema shutter controls.** Motion blur for
  zooms, pans, and the cursor now renders live on the editor canvas — during
  playback, while scrubbing, and on paused frames — matching what the export
  produces:
  - Screen zoom and pan blur runs through a real-time WebGL multi-tap
    shutter-strip shader (with an automatic Canvas 2D fallback) instead of a
    playback-only CSS blur hint.
  - Cursor motion blur accumulates up to 24 sub-frame poses along the smoothed
    cursor spline in linear premultiplied RGBA, keeping the pointer core crisp
    without ghost wash-out and tracking sub-frame zoom motion when both blur
    channels are active.
  - The **Motion Blur** card is now promoted above frame border/shadow in the
    **Layout** panel and is also available directly inside **Focus & Zoom**,
    with one-click cinema shutter presets (**Subtle 90°**, **Natural 180°**,
    **Cinematic 270°**, **Dynamic 360°**), a proportional **Strength** slider,
    a collapsible per-source **Channel mix** (**Cursor**, **Zoom**, **Pan**,
    where 50% is a 180° cinema shutter and 100% is a 360° full-frame shutter),
    and a **Preview** button that jumps the playhead to the nearest motion
    frame.

## Improvements

- **Higher-fidelity motion blur in export.** The GPU compositor now integrates
  up to 24 tapered, hardware-bilinear taps along a continuous normalized crop
  strip (up from 16 integer-snapped taps) and skips RCAS sharpening on blurred
  frames so fast sweeps never develop sharpening halos. The compatibility
  (FFmpeg) renderer now blends up to 7 tapered `zoompan` + `mix` taps (up from 5) with independent zoom and pan shutter windows. Cursor blur in both paths
  integrates up to 24 tapered midpoint-quadrature poses including click-press
  animations.
- **Glass Loupe & zoom framing polish across layouts.**
  - The Glass Loupe now stays a true circle or rounded square across portrait
    (`9:16`), square (`1:1`), padded, and side-by-side layouts in both preview
    and export.
  - When a Glass Loupe follows the cursor during an active frame zoom, the lens
    now rides the visible (zoomed) cursor tip while magnifying the unzoomed
    screen content underneath it.
  - Rounded-square lenses use an SDF-based rim distance in both preview and
    export so the glass rim highlight hugs all four edges and corners evenly
    instead of brightening the corner triangles, and the compatibility renderer
    adds a dedicated shadow margin so drop shadows fade out smoothly instead of
    clipping at the lens box.
  - The zoom target framing overlay on the canvas now supports top and bottom
    edge resize handles alongside left, right, and corner handles.

## Fixes

- **Zoom targets and Glass Loupe scale with canvas changes.** Switching canvas
  resolution tiers (for example 1080p to 4K) or changing the aspect ratio now
  rescales zoom targets and Glass Loupe centers/offsets proportionally instead
  of leaving pixel coordinates pinned toward the top-left of the new canvas.
- **Selected-range exports preserve mid-transition zooms.** Exporting a range
  that slices through a zoom or loupe transition now starts or ends from the
  exact mid-transition crop, scale, and remaining duration instead of replaying
  the full ease-in/out from 1×.
- **High-res zoom detail plane across mixed aspect ratios & NV12 grid
  alignment.** High-resolution portrait recordings in landscape canvases (and
  landscape recordings in portrait canvases) now activate the source-resolution
  zoom detail plane using the limiting fit axis. The GPU detail plane rounds up
  to the 4×2 NV12 alignment grid and caps within the 8192 px WebGPU texture
  limit, fixing an FFmpeg `pad` failure when an unpadded detail width landed on
  an even number not divisible by 4.
- **Compatibility zoom pieces preserve ramp blur.** Piecewise zoom splitting no
  longer merges motion-blurred ease-in/out ramp frames into static `Hold`
  pieces, and `evaluate_zoom_transform` now defaults an omitted `from_scale` to
  `1.0` to match the preview and `zoompan` evaluation paths.

## Internal

- `packages/editor-core/src/motion-blur.ts` centralizes cinema shutter presets,
  normalized crop-strip integration (`resolveScreenMotionBlurStrip`), and
  sub-frame cursor pose resolution (`resolveCursorMotionBlurSamples`), backed
  by `ScreenMotionBlurOverlay` and `CursorMotionBlurCanvas` in the desktop
  editor.
- `packages/cursor-engine` adds midpoint-quadrature shutter sampling with
  click-movement parity and a zero-JSON `evaluate_motion_samples_packed` WASM
  export (`packages/cursor-core/wasm/` rebuilt).
- `composite.wgsl` adds clamped bilinear texture sampling
  (`sample_screen_linear`) for motion-blur taps, tapered sine shutter weights,
  and SDF-normalized loupe rim highlights; `exports/cursor.rs` and
  `exports/camera.rs` synchronize cursor shutter poses with sub-frame zoom
  crops and decouple zoom/pan shutter windows.
- `exports/loupe.rs` and `packages/editor-core/src/zoom-loupe.ts` unify
  screen-rect and displayed-cursor loupe resolution (`resolve_loupe_in_rect` /
  `resolveLoupeAt`) and add `shadow_pad` framing to `prepare_compat_loupe`.
- Refreshed renderer-specific golden-frame fixtures (`golden/effects` and
  `golden/effects-gpu`) and added regression tests across `editor-core`,
  `cursor-core`, `media-core`, and `src-tauri/src/exports`.
- Workspace version bump to 2.5.6 across all packages; versions earlier than
  2.5.6 marked unsupported in `SECURITY.md`.
