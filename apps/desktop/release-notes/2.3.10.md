# RecordForge 2.3.10

## Fixes

- **Audio levels now match what you hear — and what you export.** Track and
  clip volume used to multiply together invisibly: pulling a track fader
  down kept the Audio panel and clip controls showing 100% while the
  actual output was quieter, and the timeline envelope drew only the clip
  half of the gain. Every surface — the Audio panel, clip inspector,
  timeline envelope, and playback preview — now shows and edits the same
  effective gain.
- **Volume automation is audible in the preview.** Dragging the audio
  envelope or its keyframes while playback was paused changed nothing you
  could hear, and envelope edits were being silently cancelled by a
  normalization bug — only mutes were audible. The preview now applies the
  same gain curve the export renders, including while paused. (Preview
  still caps at 100%, the HTML audio element's limit; export can boost
  past that.)
- **One gain control per audio source.** A track holding a single clip —
  the common case for a recorded microphone or system audio — now edits
  that clip's gain directly from the track row, so there is no hidden
  second stage. After splitting a clip the row becomes the track master
  and per-clip gains live in the clip list. Existing projects keep their
  exact sound: any legacy track fader is folded into the clips on open
  and saved back automatically.

## Internal

- `evaluateVolumeEnvelope` now scales keyframe automation by the clip's
  master volume, matching the export filter's `kf.volume * clip.volume`
  expression; `evaluateAudioGain`/`evaluatePlayheadGain` share one clamped
  gain path between preview and render plan.
- `foldTrackGainIntoTimeline` migrates legacy projects on load — track
  gain is multiplied into each clip (`clip' = clamp(clip × track)`,
  `track' = 1`) so effective gain is preserved exactly and persisted by
  the next autosave.
- The timeline envelope draws effective gain (track × clip × keyframes)
  and converts drags back to clip/keyframe values on write; keyframes are
  stored as shape multipliers so later gain changes scale the automation
  instead of flattening it.
- New `audio-gain.test.ts` suite in `@recordforge/editor-core` pins the
  envelope math, the gain fold, and the preview cap.
