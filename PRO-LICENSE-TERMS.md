## 1. What you are buying

A **license key** ("Pro key") that activates the Pro feature set in official
RecordForge desktop builds. The key is a bearer credential managed by our
merchant of record (Polar). Buying the key is a purchase of a license to
*use* the Pro features, not a purchase of the software — the software itself
is GPL-3.0.

## 2. License scope

- **Pro Lifetime**: the current price displayed at checkout; founder pricing
  may apply for a limited launch window.
- **Devices**: up to **3 personal devices** (any mix of Windows, macOS,
  Linux) activated at once. You may deactivate a device in the app
  (Settings → License → Deactivate) and re-use the slot on another device.
- **Updates**: all desktop Pro features, current and future, are included
  for the lifetime of the product — there is no per-version upgrade fee.
- **Users**: one license covers one person. Shared/team use requires a
  multi-seat pack when offered.
- **Hosted quota** (when Instant Share ships): a small hosted-share quota is
  included (planned: 10 active links, up to 10 minutes at 1080p, 30-day
  lifetime, renewable). Sharing through your own storage is unlimited and
  unaffected by the quota.

## 3. Activation and offline use

Activation requires a one-time internet connection. After that the app runs
fully offline; the app re-contacts the license server only for deactivation
and a refresh check at most once every 30 days. A network failure never
removes Pro — only an explicit revocation response does.

## 4. Refunds

**14-day, no-questions-asked refund** from the purchase date, handled by the
merchant of record. A refunded key is revoked on the next refresh check.

  use the key to activate Pro for third parties (no resale/service).
